# How to care for the leather of your mate and matera

Clean leather with a dry or slightly damp cloth, let it dry in the shade and condition it now and then with leather cream or leather grease. Direct sun and heat are what damage it most.

## Everyday cleaning

Wipe it with a dry cloth, or a slightly damp one if it has dust or a mark. Don't soak it or put it under water: too much water hardens and stains leather.

If it gets wet, let it dry in the shade, never in the sun or near a heater.

## Conditioning

Every so often, apply a little leather cream or grease with a cloth, in thin layers, and let it soak in. It brings back flexibility and protects the leather. On a mate you use every day, you will notice the difference.

## What to avoid

- **Direct sun and heat:** they dry out the leather and crack it. Don't leave it in a car in the sun.
- **Lots of water:** it hardens and stains.
- **Ordinary cleaning products:** a cloth and leather cream are enough.

## The mate: leather outside, gourd inside

A leather-covered mate needs two kinds of care: the leather's, explained here, and the gourd's (empty it, rinse it and let it dry upside down). We cover that in [how to cure a mate](/en/guides/how-to-cure-a-mate-gourd).

## Frequently asked questions

**What should I clean the leather of my mate with?**
A dry or slightly damp cloth. To condition it, leather cream or grease every so often.

**Can a leather mate get wet?**
A little water won't harm it, but don't soak it, and let it dry in the shade.

**Why does leather crack?**
Because of sun and heat. Keep it in the shade and condition it from time to time.
