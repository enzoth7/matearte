# Which mate to choose: imperial, criollo, torpedo, camionero or estribo

It depends on how you drink mate and who it is for. The **imperial** has a wide rim of worked metal and often a metal base. The **camionero** is large, with a wide mouth and a steady base. The **torpedo** is elongated, with a narrower mouth. The **criollo** has the traditional, rounder shape. And the **estribo collection** is made of gourd mates without leather, with each model's original rim.

## Imperial

The most striking one: its wide rim of worked metal is what sets it apart, and it often has a metal base. It is a gift that stands out.

This model has a family story behind it. It began on a trip Richard made to Rivera, where a handcrafted metal rim gave him the idea. The design won people over before it even had a name, until someone suggested «Mate Imperial». Today it is one of the best-known leather and gourd mates.

**Who it is for:** anyone who wants a mate that draws attention, or a special gift. It is the model most often personalized on the rim.

## Camionero

Large, with a wide mouth and a steady base. It holds more yerba and is easy to serve.

**Who it is for:** people who drink a lot of mate, and take it seriously, at the office, on the road or at home.

## Torpedo

Elongated, with a narrower mouth. It fits in one hand and is easy to carry.

**Who it is for:** anyone who prefers a slimmer mate and shorter servings.

## Criollo

The traditional, rounder shape. In the personalizer there are criollos with torpedo, imperial and camionero silhouettes, with a mate stand.

**Who it is for:** anyone looking for the classic mate, with a leather finish.

## Estribo collection

Gourd mates without leather, with the original rim of the imperial, the torpedo or the camionero.

**Who it is for:** anyone who prefers to see the natural gourd.
