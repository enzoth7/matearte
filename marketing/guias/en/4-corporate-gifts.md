# Personalized corporate gifts made in Uruguay

We make mates, materas and leather pieces with your company logo or your team's name, by hand in our Paysandú workshop. Quotes depend on quantity and design, from 30 units, and production takes 7 to 14 business days: write to us with your idea and we will send you options.

## Ideas by occasion

- **End of year:** a mate with the logo for each person on the team.
- **Anniversaries and milestones:** pieces with the name and the date, to recognize years of work or a goal reached.
- **Clients:** a Uruguayan gift with character, used every day instead of ending up in a drawer.
- **Events, conferences and visits:** a keepsake for your guests.

## How it is personalized

- **Laser engraving** on the rim: the logo or the name, in fine detail.
- **Bronze or alpaca appliqués** on the rim or the band: initials, crests or the logo in relief.
- **The mate model:** imperial, camionero, torpedo or criollo (see [which mate to choose](/en/guides/which-mate-to-choose)). Materas and leather pieces too.

Before production, our team reviews every design so it looks right on the piece.

## Experience

We have made personalized pieces for the AFA, the AUF and football stars. We are the Ortiz family, with more than 30 years of craft in Paysandú, and the brand holds the Uruguay Natural distinction.

## How to order

1. Write to us on WhatsApp or from [corporate gifts](/en/corporate-gifts) with the approximate quantity, the occasion and the logo.
2. We suggest models and techniques, with a price per unit.
3. You approve the design and we make it in the workshop.

Production takes 7 to 14 business days. For the end of the year, it is best to order 30 days in advance; if your timeline is tighter, write to us and we will work it out.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can you put my company logo on a mate?**
Yes, with laser engraving or with a bronze or alpaca appliqué, depending on the model and the design.

**Is there a minimum quantity?**
Yes, 30 units.

**How long does production take?**
7 to 14 business days from when you approve the design.

**How far in advance should I order for the end of the year?**
Ideally 30 days before. If your timeline is tighter, write to us and we will work it out.

**Do you ship nationwide and abroad?**
Yes, we ship throughout Uruguay and abroad.
